Our Founder

It Started With One Woman, One Shop, One Dream.

In 2009, inside a small shop in Ajman, a determined young woman opened the doors of what would become Shater Abbas — with more courage than capital, and one simple belief: quality fashion should be within every family's reach.

Then came the iStyle Fashion flagship in Ajman in 2020, the Company Discount Outlet in Fujairah in 2023, and a second iStyle showroom on the East Coast in 2025. From one lady and one counter — to four brands across two emirates today.

“I started with one shop and a simple belief — fashion should be for everyone.”

— In Her Words

Our Journey

From One Trading House to a Fashion Group

Four milestones that shaped who we are today — rooted in wholesale trading, growing into the emirates' favourite everyday fashion destinations.

  1. 2009

    One Woman, One Shop

    A determined young woman opens her first shop — Shater Abbas — in Ajman. Small counter, big dreams, prices for everyone.

  2. 2020

    iStyle Fashion Launches

    Opened the flagship multi-department showroom at Horizon Towers, Al Rashidiya, Ajman.

  3. 2023

    Expansion to Fujairah

    Crossed to the East Coast with the Company Discount Outlet — off-price fashion for every family.

  4. 2025

    A Second iStyle Showroom

    Opened the new iStyle Fashion Showroom in Anajaimat, Fujairah — the complete retail experience, now on the East Coast.
